# Contribution Strengthening

Improve the scientific package rather than rewriting its marketing. Preserve every accepted result
from the archived assessment pass and target the smallest additional contribution that can change the
paper route.

## Read the routed evidence

Bind every command to the exact run ID. Read `run.json.strengthening_state` and its archived:

- theory bundle and paper route;
- accepted statements and proof closure;
- novelty comparisons and limitations;
- significance blockers and routing gates;
- contribution structure and development obligations.

Identify the exact reason the requested goal is unmet. Do not treat “write more” as a scientific
plan.

## Select one strengthening thesis

Choose the highest-leverage coherent addition, in this order when applicable:

1. Complete the theorem package: add a matching lower bound, necessity result, positive companion,
   or sharpness construction.
2. Broaden the result: remove the most restrictive assumption, extend beyond a toy model, or cover a
   materially richer algorithm class.
3. Add an operational method: turn a diagnostic or bound into a justified algorithm with a theorem.
4. Differentiate from closest work: prove the exact missing regime identified by verified theorem-
   level comparisons.
5. Strengthen evidence: verify missing closest-work comparisons when novelty is the only failed gate.

Reject additions that are unrelated theorem stacking, trivial corollaries, assumed conclusions,
cosmetic reframing, or experiments invented without execution evidence.

## Rebuild affected gates

- If the controller resumes at `novelty_audit`, improve only the primary-source comparison evidence,
  then repeat novelty, significance, contribution assessment, and routing.
- If it resumes at `discovery`, state the preserved theorem as existing governed evidence and define
  a new target that complements it. Re-run exploration, proof development, both audits, Arbiter,
  novelty, significance, and contribution assessment. Re-register or re-prove preserved dependencies
  so the final bundle is self-contained.
- Use `$proof-repair` if the new theorem package later receives a repairable mathematical finding.
- Keep the original research direction fixed. Never change the central question, application domain,
  or contribution type so substantially that it becomes another paper. If such a change would be
  required, retain the current scope and complete the strongest supported route.

## Bounded autonomy

Perform at most two automatic strengthening passes. Never ask the researcher to choose among theorem
breadth, lower bounds, operational extensions, or materially different paper directions. Select the
highest-leverage in-scope addition; after the second pass, stop strengthening and route the strongest
supported package automatically.

After the bounded passes, complete routing honestly even if the goal remains unmet. Record and report:

- the strongest supported route;
- what each strengthening pass added;
- the remaining blockers;
- that the supported route was selected automatically and, for a full run, written when permitted.

Never lower correctness, novelty, or significance thresholds merely to satisfy the requested venue.
