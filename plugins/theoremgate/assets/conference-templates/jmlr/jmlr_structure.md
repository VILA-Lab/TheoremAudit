# JMLR Paper Structure

## Page budget (unlimited — typically 30-60 pages)
- Abstract: detailed, ~250 words
- Introduction: ~3-5 pages
- Related Work: ~2-3 pages (comprehensive)
- Setting and Notation: ~2-3 pages
- Main Results: ~5-10 pages
- Proofs: ~10-20 pages (full proofs in body, not appendix)
- Discussion and Open Problems: ~2-3 pages
- Conclusion: ~1 page
- References: ~3-5 pages

## Reviewer expectations
- Full proofs in the body — no "proof in appendix" shortcuts
- Comprehensive related work covering all relevant results
- Detailed discussion of assumptions and their necessity
- Open problems section expected
- Results must be substantially better than conference versions

## Theory paper tips
- JMLR is the right venue for a full version after COLT/ICLR acceptance
- Include all proof details — reviewers will check every step
- Discuss connections to adjacent fields (statistics, optimization, information theory)
- Explicit lower bounds or tightness discussions valued highly
- Notation must be completely self-contained

## Common rejection reasons
- Not substantially beyond a prior conference version
- Incomplete proofs or hand-waving in technical steps
- Missing related work in adjacent fields
- Results not tight enough for journal standards
