# TMLR Paper Structure

## Page budget (unlimited — typically 15-40 pages)
- Abstract: ~150-200 words
- Introduction: ~2-3 pages
- Related Work: ~1-2 pages
- Setting: ~1-2 pages
- Main Results: ~4-6 pages
- Discussion: ~1-2 pages
- Conclusion: ~0.5-1 page
- References: ~2-3 pages
- Appendix: proofs and additional results

## Reviewer expectations
- Rolling review — faster than conference track
- Reproducibility statement required
- Theory papers accepted without experiments
- Framework papers valued if framework is clearly useful
- Authors can revise and resubmit based on reviewer feedback

## Theory paper tips
- Good venue for borderline-novelty work that is solid and correct
- Framework papers with propositions (not just theorems) accepted
- Negative results explicitly valued by TMLR
- Reproducibility statement: for theory papers, state what would need to be reproduced
- Faster than JMLR, more flexible than conference deadlines

## Common rejection reasons
- Incremental contribution without clear advance
- Missing reproducibility statement
- Assumptions not justified
- Too similar to a prior published paper without sufficient extension
