# NeurIPS Paper Structure

## Page budget (9 pages + unlimited appendix)
- Introduction: ~1 page
- Related Work: ~0.5 page (can move to appendix)
- Setting/Preliminaries: ~1 page
- Main Results: ~2-3 pages
- Experiments/Empirical Checks: ~1-2 pages
- Conclusion: ~0.5 page
- References: ~1 page
- Appendix: full proofs, experimental details

## Reviewer expectations
- Clear motivation — why does this problem matter?
- Main result stated precisely by page 2
- At least one illustrative experiment or synthetic check
- Proof sketch in body, full proofs in appendix
- Related work must cite recent work (last 2-3 years)

## Theory paper tips
- Lead with the key novel object (e.g. A_h(λ) alignment functional)
- Use a simple example to build intuition before the formal result
- State what the result recovers as a special case (sanity check)
- Reviewers ask: is the result tight? is the assumption necessary?

## Common rejection reasons
- Missing experiments — even synthetic CPU-only checks help
- Assumptions flagged as too strong or unverifiable
- Novelty unclear relative to cited prior work
- Proof sketch too thin — must give intuition not just cite appendix
