# ICML Paper Structure

## Page budget (8 pages + unlimited appendix)
- Introduction: ~1 page
- Preliminaries: ~0.5-1 page
- Main Results: ~2-3 pages
- Experiments: ~1-2 pages (expected)
- Related Work: ~0.5 page
- Conclusion: ~0.5 page
- References: ~0.5 page
- Appendix: full proofs, extended experiments

## Reviewer expectations
- 8 pages is tight — be concise
- Empirical validation expected even for theory papers
- Proof sketches valued — don't just cite appendix
- Clear comparison to closest prior work with explicit improvement

## Theory paper tips
- State the main theorem early — don't bury it
- CPU-only synthetic experiments are sufficient for theory papers
- Explicitly state parameter dependence (H, n, δ, d)
- Recovery of known special cases is important for credibility

## Common rejection reasons
- No experimental component
- Tight page limit leads to insufficient detail
- Assumptions not compared to prior work
- Missing proof details even in appendix
