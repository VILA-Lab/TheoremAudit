# ICLR Paper Structure

## Page budget (9 pages + unlimited appendix)
- Introduction: ~1 page
- Related Work: ~0.5-1 page
- Setting and Preliminaries: ~1 page
- Main Results: ~3 pages
- Discussion/Implications: ~1 page
- Conclusion: ~0.5 page
- References: ~1 page
- Appendix: full proofs, additional results

## Reviewer expectations
- Open review — reviewers discuss publicly, authors can respond
- Theory papers fully accepted without empirical component
- Clear statement of what is new vs prior work
- Honest about limitations and open problems
- Framework papers: the framework itself must be clearly useful

## Theory paper tips
- ICLR reviewers are often more theory-friendly than NeurIPS
- Negative results and impossibility results are valued
- Be precise about assumptions — reviewers will scrutinize them
- Discussion section: explicitly state what remains open
- Address prior work conflicts head-on in the introduction

## Common rejection reasons
- Incremental contribution — must clearly exceed prior work
- Assumptions not justified or too bespoke
- Related work missing key references
- Claims not fully supported by proofs in appendix
