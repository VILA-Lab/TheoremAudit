# COLT 2026 Formatting Notes

Source: https://learningtheory.org/colt2026/cfp.html, verified 2026-08-14 UTC.

- Use `\documentclass[anon,12pt]{colt2026}` for review and
  `\documentclass[final,12pt]{colt2026}` only for the camera-ready version.
- Submit one PDF suitable for double-blind review.
- The main body is limited to 12 PMLR-formatted pages.
- References and appendices are excluded from the 12-page limit.
- Put appendices after the references in the same PDF.
- Include all details, proofs, and derivations needed to substantiate the results, in the main body
  or appendix.
- Include enough proof detail in the main body for reviewers to assess correctness, novelty, and
  significance without relying entirely on the appendix.
- Omit author names, identifying information, and acknowledgments during review.
- Refer to prior work, including the authors' own work, in the third person.

These notes summarize the captured 2026 instructions. Recheck the official page before submission.
